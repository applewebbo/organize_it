# Collaboration

Organize It lets you collaborate on trips with other users. The trip owner can invite registered users — or people without an account yet — to join a trip as collaborators.

## What is Collaboration?

When you add a collaborator to a trip:

- They gain **full edit access** to the trip (events, stays, days, links)
- Their contributions are marked with a **colored badge** on events and stays
- They can see the trip in their **"Shared with me"** section on the homepage
- The trip owner retains ownership and can remove collaborators at any time

## Adding a Collaborator

You can add a collaborator directly from the trip detail page.

### Adding a Registered User

1. Open the trip detail page
2. Click the **Manage Collaborators** button in the trip header
3. In the search field, type the collaborator's email address (partial matches are supported)
4. Select the user from the results
5. Click **Add**

The collaborator is immediately added and receives an **email notification**.

!!! info "Color Assignment"
    Each collaborator is automatically assigned a unique color from a palette (blue, green, purple, orange, pink, teal, red, indigo). This color appears as a badge on events and stays they create, making it easy to see who contributed what.

### Inviting a Non-Registered User

If the person you want to collaborate with does not have an Organize It account yet:

1. Open the trip detail page
2. Click **Manage Collaborators**
3. Enter their email address in the search field
4. If no user is found, click **Invite by email**
5. An invitation email is sent to that address with a link to join

!!! info "Invitation Expiry"
    Invitations are valid for **7 days**. After that, the link expires and a new invitation must be sent.

### Accepting an Invitation

When a non-registered user receives an invitation email:

1. Click the link in the email
2. If they don't have an account, they are redirected to the **sign-up page**
3. After registering (or logging in if they already have an account), they are automatically added as a collaborator to the trip

## Removing a Collaborator

1. Open the trip detail page
2. Click **Manage Collaborators**
3. Click the **×** button next to the collaborator you want to remove
4. The collaborator is immediately removed and loses access to the trip

!!! warning "Access Revoked Immediately"
    Once removed, the collaborator can no longer view or edit the trip.

## Collaborator Badges

Each collaborator is assigned a color that appears as a small badge on events and stays they created. This makes it easy to see at a glance who added what to the trip.

The trip owner is shown with a distinct badge labeled **"Owner"**.

## Email Notifications

Collaborators receive email notifications when:

- They are **added** to a trip
- They are **removed** from a trip
- A new **event or stay** is added to a trip they collaborate on

!!! tip "Language Preference"
    Notification emails are sent in the language set in your account preferences. See [Account Settings](../getting-started/quick-start.md) for how to change your language.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a collaborator invite other users?

No. Only the trip **owner** can add or remove collaborators.

### Can a collaborator delete the trip?

No. Only the **owner** can delete or archive a trip.

### What can a collaborator do?

Collaborators have the same edit permissions as the owner for trip content (events, stays, days, links), but cannot delete the trip, change its dates, or manage other collaborators.

### What happens to events created by a removed collaborator?

Events and stays created by a removed collaborator remain on the trip. Only their colored badge is updated.
